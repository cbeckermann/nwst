Wild Atlantic Way — Inishowen, Donegal

Discover the breathtaking beauty of the Inishowen Peninsula on this unforgettable full-day guided tour along the spectacular Wild Atlantic Way. Travel through dramatic mountain scenery, historic sites, and rugged Atlantic coastline.

  • Fort Dunree Military Museum
  • Malin Head — Ireland's most northerly point
  • Doagh Famine Village experience
  • Pub lunch at Nancy's Barn, Ballyliffin
