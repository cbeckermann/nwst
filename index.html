Our Story

Rooted in the North West

North West Scenic Tours was founded in 2012 by Donegal native Ciarán O'Donnell with a simple mission: share the wild beauty of Ireland's north-west with the world. What started as a single minibus and a passion for storytelling has grown into Ireland's most loved regional tour operator.

Our guides aren't just drivers — they're historians, storytellers, and naturalists who grew up in the landscapes they lead you through. Every tour is a conversation, not a commentary.
